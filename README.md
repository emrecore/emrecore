## About Me

I am interested in the intersection of **healthcare, data, statistics, and research**, with a particular focus on how quantitative methods can support better understanding and decision-making in complex real-world environments.

I enjoy transforming data into structured insights, questioning how information is represented, and examining the assumptions and limitations behind statistical results. My projects often combine **data analysis, statistical reasoning, programming, and scientific interpretation**.

Beyond individual methods or technologies, I am especially interested in problems involving **healthcare analytics, digital health, medical data, and evidence-based decision-making**.

I continuously develop my analytical and technical skills through practical projects, independent research, and the exploration of new quantitative methods.

My goal is to build work that is not only technically sound, but also **clear, reproducible, interpretable, and relevant to real-world problems**.
